📊 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI, based on the Adventure Works 2020 dataset. The dashboard provides end-to-end business intelligence coverage across five analytical dimensions — from high-level KPIs to granular customer and territory insights.
The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making across sales, product management, marketing, and finance functions.

🔑 Key Business Insights

High Revenue Concentration — Bikes account for ~97% of total revenue, posing a category concentration risk.
Strong Profitability — A 41.15% overall profit margin demonstrates effective cost management.
Accessories Are Most Profitable — Despite low revenue share, Accessories deliver a 63% margin.
Near-Budget Performance — 98.38% budget attainment reflects accurate forecasting and execution.
Australia is a Top Market — Comparable to the US in revenue, making it a key growth region.
Balanced Gender Distribution — Near-equal revenue from male and female customers.
